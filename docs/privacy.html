Med Note — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Med Note is a study app for medical students (notes, flashcards, lecture recording, calendar, groups, and an optional AI assistant). This policy describes what data the app uses and why.

Data we process

  • Account. If you sign in with email, we store your email, display name, and a PBKDF2 hash of your password. The password itself is not stored or sent in plaintext. Sign in with Apple / Google uses the identifier provided by that service.
  • Notes and study data. Notes, photos, flashcards, lecture audio/transcripts, calendar events, chats, and groups stay on your device and, when you are signed in and iCloud is enabled, sync via Apple iCloud / CloudKit.
  • Device list. After sign-in we store a device name and a random device id so you can see which devices used the account.
  • Optional AI. If you add your own API key in settings, prompts you send (note text, chat questions, transcripts) go to that provider (default: OpenAI). We do not see or store your API key on our servers — it stays in the device Keychain.

Permissions

  • Microphone and speech recognition — to record and transcribe lectures.
  • Camera and photo library — to add slides and photos to notes.
  • Notifications — calendar reminders you enable.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your data for tracking or advertising.
  • We do not require an account: you can skip sign-in and use the app locally.

Third parties

Apple iCloud/CloudKit processes synced data under Apple’s terms. If you enable live AI, the provider you configure processes the text you send. Google Sign-In is used only if you choose that sign-in method.

Retention and deletion

You can sign out or delete the account from Profile. Local notes remain on the device until you delete them. Removing the app deletes local data. iCloud copies follow Apple’s iCloud deletion rules when you delete records or the account.

Children

Med Note is intended for university students and is not directed at children under 13.

Medical disclaimer

Content in the app is for studying. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or a medical device.

Политика конфиденциальности Med Note

Med Note — приложение для студентов-медиков: конспекты, карточки, запись лекций, календарь, группы и ИИ-ассистент.

Если вы входите по почте, хранятся адрес, имя и хеш пароля (сам пароль открытым текстом не сохраняется). Конспекты, фото, карточки и записи лекций лежат на устройстве и при входе синхронизируются через iCloud. Данные не продаём и не используем для рекламы. Вход можно пропустить.
